## Quick Start Guide

### If you're a linguist:
1. Start with [Getting Started](generated/linguist-getting-started.md) to create your account
2. Export your texts from FLEx using FlexTools
3. Follow [Uploading Your Project](generated/linguist-uploading-project.md)
4. Use [Sharing Your Project](generated/linguist-sharing-project.md) to collaborate
5. When ready, see [Downloading Results](generated/linguist-downloading-results.md)

### If you're a community member:
1. You'll receive a link from your linguist
2. Follow [Accessing a Project](generated/community-accessing-project.md)
3. Use [Adding Translations](generated/community-adding-translations.md) to contribute
