# Getting Started - Sign Up and Log In

This guide will help you create an account and log into the FLEX Interlinear Translation web application.

## What You'll Need

- A valid email address
- A secure password
- Internet connection

## Step 1: Open the Web Application

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
2. Navigate to the web application URL provided by your administrator
3. You should see the login page with "FLEX Interlinear Translation" at the top

## Step 2: Create Your Account

If this is your first time using the application:

1. Look for the **"Sign In / Sign Up"** button on the page
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field
3. Create a secure password and enter it in the "Password" field
4. Click the **"Sign In / Sign Up"** button

**Note:** The same button is used for both signing up and logging in. The system will automatically create a new account if your email doesn't exist yet.

## Step 3: Verify Your Email

After signing up:

1. Check your email inbox for a confirmation message
2. Look for an email from the web application
3. Click the verification link in the email
4. This confirms your email address is valid

**Tip:** Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see the email within a few minutes.

## Step 4: Log In

Once your email is verified:

1. Return to the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click **"Sign In / Sign Up"**
5. You'll be taken to your linguist dashboard

## Troubleshooting

### "I didn't receive a confirmation email"
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait a few minutes - emails can be delayed
- Make sure you entered your email address correctly
- Try signing up again

### "I forgot my password"
- Contact your administrator for password reset assistance

### "The page won't load"
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Try a different web browser
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
